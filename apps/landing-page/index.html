FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can’t find what you need? Email support@mytix.com.au and we’ll get back to you.

How do I get my tickets after booking?

All Mytix tickets are digital. Once payment is confirmed, your tickets appear in My Tickets in the app, and you’ll receive a confirmation email with your e-ticket and QR code.

How does entry work at the venue?

Open your ticket in the app (or your confirmation email) and tap Scan this code at the gate. Venue staff scan your QR code and you’re in — no printing needed.

Can I transfer or cancel my tickets?

Depending on the organiser’s policy for the event, you can share a pass with a friend from the ticket screen, or request a cancellation from your profile up to 24 hours before the event starts.

What happens if an event is postponed or cancelled?

If an event is rescheduled, your tickets stay valid for the new date. If it’s cancelled, or you can’t make the new date, a full refund goes back to your original payment method, usually within 5–7 business days.

Which payment methods can I use?

You can pay with Visa, Mastercard or American Express, with a digital wallet, or with Afterpay. Payments are processed securely by Stripe in Australian dollars.

Are there any hidden fees?

No. Booking fees and GST are shown line by line on the checkout screen before you pay, so the total you see is the total you’re charged.
